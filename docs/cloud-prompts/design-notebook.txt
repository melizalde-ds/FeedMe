## The soul to keep

FeedMe helps busy households eat safely and well, whatever their allergies, diet
or budget. It should feel warm, homely and a little hand-made: cream paper, food,
hand-drawn green arrows and circled notes, a script flourish, a friendly rounded
logo, and the words on the mood board, "clean simple". Wherever allergies are
concerned it is calm and serious, never playful.

## Fixed

- Colors: the hex values of the tokens, in light and in dark. No new hues. Tints
  made from the existing tokens with color-mix are fine.
- The three logo files, unedited: no recolor, redraw or crop. Choose which one
  fits each place.
- A script or handwritten accent font, used sparingly.
- Every feature, and the journey Setup, then Plan, then Grocery.
- Light and dark themes, with the toggle.
- This is the app, not a landing page. The welcome popup stays a popup: no hero
  and no marketing sections anywhere.

## Open

- Where each color goes. The green header bar, the yellow table cells and the
  rest of today's placement are not to be preserved: decide again which color
  carries what.
- The heading and body typefaces.
- Navigation, page composition and component shapes.

## Screens

The welcome popup, login, register, the save-your-setup prompt after sign-up,
Setup (household, allergies, diet, nutrition goals, budget), Plan (week tabs,
plan table, summary, recipe detail and modal, swap picker, my recipes, discover
and shared plans), Grocery (list and checkout panel), the guest and signed-in
header, and the safety disclaimer.

## Rules

- Tailwind across the board. Every component is styled with utilities. When you
  finish, index.css holds only the Tailwind import, the tokens, the theme
  mapping and base styles: no component classes.
- Visual layer only. Do not change src/lib, the providers' logic, the hooks, the
  API client, auth or config. If the design needs a behavior change, describe it
  in the pull request and leave the code as it is.
- Images. The repo has no food photos. Do not hotlink, download or generate any.
  When a recipe has `imageUrl`, render it lazily at a fixed aspect ratio and
  fall back if it fails to load. Otherwise show a designed photo slot.
- Planner meals have no prices and no ingredient amounts. Design for that
  absence. Do not fill it.
- Planning takes 25 to 35 seconds. Design a waiting state worth looking at.
- Design the empty, loading, error and session-expired states of every screen.
- WCAG AA contrast in both themes, visible focus, full keyboard use, reduced
  motion respected, and a layout that works at 360px wide.
- The two dark-theme blocks in index.css disagree on `--green`. Make them
  consistent and say in the pull request which value you kept.
- public/favicon.svg is empty. Make a favicon from pic-logo.png.
- No new runtime dependencies beyond fonts, unless the pull request says why.
